First release.

Dawnbreak is an alarm clock you stop by completing a mission: twelve of them, four difficulties, up to ten rounds. Stop it without the mission and it rings again a minute later. Twelve languages. Free: nothing to buy, no account, no ads, and nothing leaves your phone.

If something is wrong, Settings has a “Contact support” link that opens an email, and the support page is linked on this page. Messages get read.
