Dawnbreak collects nothing. There is no account to create, no server to talk to, and no analytics of any kind. This page is short because there is very little to describe.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, the missions attached to them, your preferences and your wake-up history are written to files inside the app's own container on your device. They are never uploaded. If you back your device up to iCloud or to a computer, they are part of that backup, which is between you and Apple.

The camera and the motion sensors

Three missions use the camera: photographing an object, scanning a barcode, and counting squats. Frames are analysed on the device, in memory, while the mission is on screen, and are discarded as they are replaced. Nothing is written to your photo library and nothing is transmitted. Two missions read the motion sensors to count steps or detect a shake; those readings are used as they arrive and never stored.
